CampusRent Help Centre

CampusRent is a student-to-student marketplace for borrowing and lending useful campus items. Find something you need for classes or exams, or list an item you own and earn rent when another student borrows it.

How to create an account

1

Open Sign up

Visit the CampusRent home page and select Sign up in the top navigation.

2

Enter your details

Provide your full name, email address and a password with at least 8 characters. You can also add your college or university name.

3

Verify your email

Select the sign-up button to receive a one-time password (OTP) by email. Check your inbox and spam folder, enter the 6-digit OTP, and submit it.

4

Start using CampusRent

Once your email is verified, your account is created. Log in with your email and password whenever you return.

Keep your password private. Never share an email OTP or password with anyone.

How to rent an item

1

Browse available items

Explore the home page, use categories or search to find calculators, stationery, drafting tools, lab equipment, books, electronics and other listed items.

2

Check the listing

Open an item to review its photos, condition, daily rent, refundable deposit (if any), and pickup location.

3

Send a rental request

Choose your rental dates and submit a request. The item owner can accept or decline it. A request is not confirmed until the owner accepts it.

4

Coordinate and return

Use Messages to contact the owner and agree on handover details. Return the item as agreed and coordinate with the owner to complete the return.

How to list an item and earn

1

Select List an item

Log in and choose List an item.

2

Add clear details

Enter the item name, category, condition, daily rent, refundable deposit (if applicable), pickup spot and description. Add up to five clear photos.

3

Manage requests

Open My space to review incoming rental requests. Accept or decline requests and use Messages to coordinate with the renter.

4

Mark the item returned

After the item is physically returned, mark the rental as returned in your account so the listing can become available again.

What else can I do?

My space

Manage your listings, incoming requests, outgoing rentals and rental activity from one place.

Messages

Chat with other students to discuss item details, pickup, handover and return arrangements.

Ratings

Where available, share a rating after a rental has been completed and the item returned.

College discovery

CampusRent is designed for students across colleges. Add your college to your profile to help show relevant campus listings.

Important things to know

CampusRent helps students discover items and manage rental requests. Students should agree directly on handover, payment, deposits and return arrangements. Payments are not collected or held by CampusRent. Always inspect an item at handover, agree on its condition and terms before taking it, and meet in a safe, suitable campus location.

CampusRent is currently in a testing phase, so features and the interface may change. If something does not work, return to the website and share the issue with the CampusRent team.
