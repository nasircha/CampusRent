    How it works

    1. 1

      List your gear

      Add a photo, a daily rent and a refundable deposit. It takes about a minute.

    2. 2

      Request and approve

      A borrower picks the days and a pickup date. You accept or decline.

    3. 3

      Hand over and return

      Meet on campus, settle rent and deposit in person, then mark the item returned.
